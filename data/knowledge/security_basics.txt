Security Basics

Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication for work accounts. Do not upload confidential documents to public AI services without authorization. Treat retrieved documents and user-uploaded files as untrusted input. A retrieval-augmented generation system should ground answers in retrieved evidence, show source references, and clearly state when the evidence is insufficient.

Applications should avoid logging passwords, access tokens, personal data, or document contents unnecessarily. Store secrets in environment variables during local development and use a managed secrets service in production. Restrict document access to authorized users and validate uploaded file types and sizes.
