Company Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted through the employee portal at least five working days before the planned leave, except in emergencies. Managers approve requests based on team coverage and business requirements. Unused annual leave carryover is governed by the employee's employment agreement and applicable company rules; employees should confirm their individual balance with Human Resources.

Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible. For absences lasting more than three consecutive working days, employees may be asked to provide supporting documentation according to company policy. This sample policy is illustrative and is not legal or official HR guidance.
